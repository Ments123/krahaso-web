For a time-series analysis, request the first and latest observation dates, product matching rules and coverage for the retailers you need.

How Krahaso records prices

An observation is a price recorded from a tracked retailer source. A single product can contribute observations at several retailers or on several dates. We collect observations daily from sources such as retailer price lists, online catalogues and promotional leaflets.

Ask for the price basis, observation dates, retailer scope and product matching rules in your sample. Those details matter when comparing regular and promotional prices, different pack sizes or markets with different currencies.

Questions about licensing

Who can license Krahaso Data?

Can we test the grocery price API?

Request a sample for the markets, products and dates you need. We’ll confirm the data fields and integration steps and arrange API key access for licensed partners.

What does the basket preview show?

Five rounded weekly points from the Kosovo Everyday Basket Index. The index tracks regular prices of 15 staples relative to a base of 100 on 29 July 2026. Detailed history and extracts are available under licence.
