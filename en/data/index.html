Questions about licensing

Who can license Krahaso Data?

Krahaso works with brands, retailers, distributors, research teams and institutions. Tell us your intended use so we can confirm coverage, access and commercial terms.

Can we test the grocery price API?

Request a sample for the markets, products and dates you need. We’ll confirm the data fields and integration steps and arrange API key access for licensed partners.

What does the basket preview show?

Five rounded weekly readings from Kosovo and Albania, indexed to 100 on 20 October 2026. A commercial licence can include daily history and product-level extracts.
